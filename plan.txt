PHASE 3c - MATCH-PREVIEW SCHEDULED POSTS (per user)
=====================================================================
Goal: a SEPARATE, second post type. At 00:00 UTC daily the bot fetches today's
PL fixtures and SCHEDULES a comment-bait post 5 hours before each kickoff
(regular Facebook post with real club crests - NOT a reel).

DECISIONS (all locked 2026-09-03):
- Regular Facebook post (image + caption), NOT a reel.
- Ran at MIDNIGHT (00:00 UTC), independent of the hourly news cron.
- Source: football-data.org v4 free tier (PL, code "PL"), X-Auth-Token header.
- Prints real club crests from crests.football-data.org/<id>.png on a VS card.
- Schedules via Graph API /feed with published=false + scheduled_publish_time
  (BOTH required together - if published defaults true the schedule is silently
  ignored and it posts immediately). Window: 10 min .. 30 days out.
- Scheduled to fire `MATCH_POST_HOURS_BEFORE` (=5) hours before kickoff.
- Skips kickoffs less than 5h away (avoid posting a match about to start).
- DEDUP: each fixture posts once (match_key HOMETLA-vs-AWYTLA-DATE in manifest).
