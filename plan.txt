  actual posts/day ≈ number of fresh PL stories, not a fixed cadence.
  ALSO added FUZZY cross-source dedup: story_similar() requires (a) shared
  player/club proper-noun AND (b) SequenceMatcher ratio >= config.DEDUP_THRESHOLD
  (0.30). This prevents BBC/Sky/Guardian/FourFourTwo covering ONE story from being
  posted multiple times. Applied in select_postable() against the post log AND the
  current fresh batch (one run never posts two sources of the same story).

  07:00-22:00 UK; shift cron to "0 8-23 * * *" then. Review in late Oct.
